## Test intent and test ethics
Verify that ORIGINAL **Part3** can contact at least two different eligible player identities in the **same public MM2 server**, classify normal ignore/decline and accept, and hand at least one accepted original trade root to ORIGINAL **Part4**, which automatically verifies no MAIN item is being offered and safely Declines. No one needs to press final Accept. There is no exchange of weapons, market decision, profit, Discord, server hop or claim of game-server settlement.

This is a **bounded normal public-server trade-request test**, not high-volume outreach. Only request players with independently scanner-confirmed Ancient/Godly/Chroma inventories, and up to **three distinct UserIds per one operator ARM**. A successful public request is not a person's consent to hand over items, and no public player's response is guaranteed. No duplicate taps on an uncertain send. Using Delta/modified Roblox client may violate game rules/anti-cheat and risk account sanctions; operator chooses whether to run.
