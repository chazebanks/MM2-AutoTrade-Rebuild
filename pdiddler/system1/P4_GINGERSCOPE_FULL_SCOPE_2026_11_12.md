## Scope and ownership — a cohesive P4 workflow, not duplicating P3

**P3 remains the contact/queue owner:** identify eligible Ancient/Godly/Chroma inventory holders, ignore/decline/cancel, avoid duplicate requests, select next eligible player, track joins/leaves and optional safe server navigation. P3 pauses on one guarded exact P3→P4 session and resumes when the current P4 lease is explicitly released after a verified close. Never embed a second P3 scanner, request rotation, or ignored-request timeout inside P4.

**P4 must orchestrate ALL of the following**, regardless of whether the computations are supplied by separate System2/System3 libraries:

1. **Trade/session state:** bind exact P3 partner/epoch, physical GUI root, both actual offers, variant/year/Chroma, physical slot count versus copies, quantity, acceptance state and offer revision (unknown proof stays UNKNOWN). Never use historical display-name-only mapping as authenticated raw item identity.
2. **Portfolio-aware Gingerscope growth planner (core requirement, not optional external side project):** maintain own actual inventory, investable inventory vs protected items, total verified listed value, usable per-item demand/stability/liquidity/retradability data, item concentration and portfolio attractiveness. Evaluate **small compounding improvements**, upgrades (multiple lesser items → stronger/desirable item), downgrades (stronger item → several retradable items, appropriately compensated), sidegrades, inventory recycling, risk of hoarding unpopular items, and longer-term feasibility of trading for Gingerscope. Decide whether to wait for ALT's offer, open with a sensible eligible item, counteroffer, increase/decrease offer, walk away or pursue a direct Gingerscope acquisition. Never impose obsolete universal `+15` floor or mandatory highest-value opener.
3. **Price and market provenance firewall:** join exact verified item-key mappings to truly current publisher-authenticated listed values (Supreme/MM2Values), demand, stability, independently corroborated practical retradeability/liquidity, updated timestamps and source disagreements. Missing/stale prices and fake liquidity are UNKNOWN, never 'safe' or silently Stable. Do not use stale scanner `inv.total` as trusted trading authority. Market capture/network calls should be decoupled from P4's active in-game critical section.
4. **Offer and counteroffer execution:** generate a bounded four-PHYSICAL-slot plan, including quantities/stacked copies; present a feasible proposal attractive to the other player *as well as* beneficial to our inventory. Re-verify current live GUI/partner/inventory and plan before/between/after every placement and removal, detect active ALT mutations and ABA/stale shadows, withdraw/replan or decline safely. Don't leave obsolete outgoing items offered. Preserve one move at a time, deadlines, state locks, invalidation, deduplication, and fail-stop on uncertainty.
5. **Bounded strategic risk:** weigh listed-value gain AND quality-adjusted exit inventory; prefer no-loss alternative when attractive. Long-term policy allows *considering* controlled listed-value-loss upgrades into exceptionally better, liquid Gingerscope-enabling inventory, but actual loss execution **requires separate verified evidence, numeric risk budget and explicit deployment approval**. Existing `strategy/growth_policy.json` has ALL numeric caps unapproved and live loss deployment disabled.
6. **Offer acceptance / state reconciliation:** observe opponent acceptance/last-offer indicators and two-step exchange states; invalidate approvals on ANY offer mutation, identity/partner/session change, unexpected UI close or ambiguity. Automatic final Accept is still OFF pending independent completion and rollout gates. Separate client GUI close, client accept, transaction result UNKNOWN and **server-confirmed settlement**.
7. **Private Discord notifications / comprehensive trading telemetry (explicit P4 responsibility):** on each eligible **independently verified settled trade**, publish a privacy-safe, **idempotent** success receipt through a trusted dispatcher with actual items + quantities given and received, validated comparable source values/timestamps, item demand/stability/liquidity profile, item-attractiveness changes, gain/loss (separate raw and quality-adjusted), updated inventory total and key quality/target-progress metrics, counterparty/session/outcome evidence classification, unique deduplication/event key and verified/unverified distinction. A separate explicitly labeled private **diagnostic** event stream may record offers/revisions/declines/unknown outcomes without profit/settled claims, to support denominator research; do not claim these are completed trades, and do not leak webhook secrets/tokens/usernames to public GitHub/CI or reports. Never send a *completed-trade profit notification* just because a client window closed.
8. **Experience and learning feedback:** create private structured evidence across offers attempted, ALT counteroffers, withdrawals, declines, timeouts, unattractive proposals and independently confirmed settlements; exclude consenting test ALT from real learning samples; quarantine ambiguous/self-reported Discord records. **System3 learns from P4's verified private evidence**, evaluates alternatives and calibrates probabilities of acceptance, value realization, retradeability and time-to-exit. It must not adapt live economic policy from unverified client events or fake acceptance denominators; use held-out validation, measured drift and rollout/rollback gates. Discord is a notification/ledger transport and **not itself proof** that a trade settled. Rejected/unknown outcomes may eventually inform *separate* behavioral models with valid attribution; never label them a completed trade or profitable sale.
9. **P3 resume and operations:** after verified trade close/decline, free only the exact P4 lease, produce a classified outcome/receipt, then ask P3 to resume the next player. Track failure modes, safety counters and controlled recovery in the full unattended system; P3 owns actual target rotation and server-hop policy. Operator STOP still preempts any trade execution.
