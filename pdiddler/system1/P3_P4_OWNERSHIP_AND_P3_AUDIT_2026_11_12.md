## One actual P3 safety bug fixed under this draft

Previously, the 15-second ignored-request timeout returned `"ignored"` even when `cancelPending()` returned `false` (pending widget could remain). That could permit a second contact while the first outgoing request remained unresolved. In the **reviewed P3 fork only**, the new path calls `cancelPending()`, checks a fresh `pendingVisible()` result and that no trade opened after cancel, then marks `ignored` **only when cancellation is client-verified**.

If canceled-state proof fails, mark `sendfailed`, record the specific reason, set sticky `failStop`, close `startGateOpen`, pause rotation, and deny re-arm/resume. Do not retry the uncertain request, decline an unbound window or contact another target.
