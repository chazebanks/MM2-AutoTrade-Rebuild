# Gingerscope supervised validation gates

The consolidated candidate qualifies the controlled desktop sequence. Milestone B requires separately reviewed supervised evidence. Milestone C requires a release decision on the exact operating scope. Use the [requirements matrix](gingerscope_requirements.json), [dependency map](GINGERSCOPE_DEPENDENCY_MAP.md), and [release gates](../RELEASE_READINESS.json) to trace each decision.

The project has a supported desktop environment for Python, SQLite and Lune. It has historical MM2 client receipts and historical client-only launchers. It has no reviewed, supported interface that connects the current candidate to the live MM2 client, exposes authoritative offer revisions and item identity, and produces an independent settlement receipt. Opening that gate requires an approved integration capability, an identified owner of the authoritative data, and a separate authorized test scope. The records below define admission and observation criteria for a future supervised test. They grant no game access or trading authority.

## 1. Admit the next Supreme publication

- **Unresolved capability.** Current-clock pricing after the reviewed archive cutoff, including verified additions, removals and item mappings under a new publisher edition.
- **Supported environment.** Use the existing official Supreme capture command and independent review on the desktop. The November 25 archive has a fixed cutoff of 12:09:59 UTC and remains suitable for explicit historical replay.
- **Participation and starting conditions.** A source reviewer records the new publication timestamp, official page URLs and raw page hashes. Preserve the previous archive, its original receipt and the exact code revision. Start with an independently observed, genuinely newer official publication.
- **Expected result.** The reader admits the reviewed capture at a permitted clock, reports the correct census and changes, and retires evaluations tied to the earlier edition. A local report identifies the prior source and the new source separately.
- **Failure and stop.** An unchanged edition after cutoff, missing source pages, mismatched timestamps or fingerprints, or unresolved identity provenance leaves the current-price gate closed. Keep the historical fixture clock fixed.
- **Evidence and limits.** Retain raw publisher hashes, timestamps and a public census or approved capture receipt. This establishes source identity, freshness and descriptive prices for an edition. Actual traded prices, willingness, liquidity and outcomes remain separate evidence.

## 2. Qualify a supported native host and session handoff

- **Unresolved capability.** Current P1–P4 initialization, single-owner handoff and STOP/COPY behavior for the present integration contract on the intended device.
- **Supported environment.** A separately approved observation interface and exact candidate build on the intended supported client and operating system are prerequisites. The desktop test can qualify its own runtime, startup and handoff.
- **Participation and starting conditions.** One operator and one consenting participant in a controlled session. Record the exact candidate revision and compatibility manifest, client build, supported observation interface, initial view, and owner state. Start with no active trade or competing controller and with the stop control available. Use non-protected test holdings and ordinary participant-controlled actions within the authorized scope.
- **Expected result.** The host declares dependencies and readiness, binds one current session to one owner, enters the observer stage, and releases the owner when stopped. Duplicate ownership and stale handoffs fail closed. The operator can copy a sanitized receipt from a terminal or safely stopped state.
- **Failure and stop.** Unavailable or unreviewed interface, unexpected activity, mismatched binding, lost stop control or identity ambiguity ends the observation. Record the first failed stage and its enum, and leave outcomes unresolved.
- **Evidence and limits.** Record startup/stop transitions, owner-generation changes, and the paired controlled result. On a target device, record elapsed times and available resource measures for this exact workload. These results cover the tested client build and device. Server settlement, general device performance and operation under other host versions remain separate gates.

## 3. Corroborate exact native inventory and changing-offer binding

- **Unresolved capability.** Attribute current-candidate native observations to exact items, physical slots, quantities, a session and an offer revision. Demonstrate the new bridge boundary and advisory invalidation beyond the historical single-flow receipts.
- **Supported environment.** Reuse the approved observation interface and candidate established by Gate 2. A source exposing documented identity and revision semantics is required to certify authoritative identity or offer revision.
- **Participation and starting conditions.** Use the same consented session without completing an exchange. Begin with a recorded empty or known baseline, separate main and counterparty inventories, two resolved non-protected test identities, and a separately identified ambiguous-observation case. If stacked lots are in scope, predeclare one known stack and its actual displayed slot. Preserve the separate copy and slot meanings.
- **Expected result.** A participant-controlled change yields a new, session-bound observation. The prior evaluation becomes historical and the current recommendation is recomputed against the same reviewed market edition and unchanged account ownership. Unresolved aliases, quantity inconsistencies, wrong owners and unsupported observations stop the candidate from offering advice for that revision.
- **Failure and stop.** Stop on mismatched identity, inconsistent copies, unidentified session, stale advice, unexpected game action or loss of observation provenance. A client-only token is retained at its observed tier.
- **Evidence and limits.** Keep sanitized counts for distinct slots, copies, revision changes and stale-result denials. Review exact binding and source identifiers privately when authorized. This tests identity, revisions and advisory handoff at the proved source tier. Consent, backend completion and actual market willingness require separate evidence.

## 4. Admit an independently corroborated outcome

- **Unresolved capability.** Obtain authority for game-backed outcome classification and bilateral reconciliation against the exact revision, with duplicate handling through restart.
- **Supported environment.** A reviewed source owner must first establish an independently trustworthy, transaction-scoped outcome and inventory provenance for both sides. An operator-authorized controlled transaction, if needed, requires a separately approved scope for both participants and the approved host.
- **Participation and starting conditions.** Establish the source's identity and replay semantics, exact authorized scope, transaction/session linkage, the current market edition, a bound offer revision and before-state for both sides. Choose ordinary non-protected test holdings and a reviewed no-loss scope. Participants retain control of all final actions.
- **Expected result.** An independently corroborated terminal event reconciles against matching before/after changes on both sides and at most one accounting entry is recorded after redelivery or restart. A decline, interruption, UI closure or ambiguous revision remains at its evidence tier. Record whether each outcome was eligible for accounting and whether a separate learning gate admitted it.
- **Failure and stop.** Missing independent authority, duplicate transaction ID with conflicting contents, stale terms, missing corroboration, inconsistent deltas or operator stop leaves the outcome unresolved or rejected according to the evidence contract. Preserve the already reviewed record without taking another action to obtain a cleaner receipt.
- **Evidence and limits.** Authorized private records need the exact source, transaction/session binding, revision, timestamp, both parties' exact permitted deltas and deduplication result. Export only sanitized status and counts publicly. One such case can verify the contract for the recorded transaction. Population liquidity, broader settlement reliability and unattended release require further evidence.

## 5. Evaluate empirical outcomes and real learning eligibility

- **Unresolved capability.** Measure legitimate offer-level response and later retradability, and evaluate real outcome learning without leaking identities, groups or future information.
- **Supported environment.** A separately authorized private collection and analysis path with permitted access to consented, independently corroborated offer and settlement evidence. The existing private client-only V2 archive and the synthetic learner remain useful qualification paths within their evidence tiers.
- **Participation and starting conditions.** Participants or an authorized data steward supply already-permitted observations from ordinary trading. Predeclare eligible events, group identity representation, sampling period, ambiguity exclusions, missing outcome handling and the minimum evaluation evidence. Preserve a temporally later holdout with no participant/session overlap.
- **Expected result.** Each eligible row links to a legitimate timed outcome, a decision-time feature version and an independent observation source. The run reports denominators, attrition, calibration or an abstention, a fixed-baseline comparison, and an independent later portfolio-quality check. Report measured retradability only where follow-up corroboration exists.
- **Failure and stop.** Exclude repeated test gifts, unattributed client events, inconsistent source identity, duplicate records and rows with unavailable decision-time features. Abstain when cohort size, temporal integrity, coverage or corroboration fails. Store raw identities, messages and sensitive portfolio details only within approved private custody.
- **Evidence and limits.** Report data scope, provenance, exclusion counts, aggregate outcomes, uncertainty, held-out metrics and versioned reproducibility material. A representative sample supports a bounded measurement for that population and time. Future markets and other participants remain unverified.

## Release decision

Milestone C requires exact revisions and passing qualification artifacts, admitted current-market provenance, evidence for the authorized host and outcome claims, a reviewed privacy and recovery path, and an explicit operator release decision. Any future numerical listed-loss policy requires separate evidence and approval. Until those decisions are recorded, the supported deliverable is controlled qualification and advisory analysis. Archive only sanitized evidence outside private custody, and keep the candidates as open drafts.
