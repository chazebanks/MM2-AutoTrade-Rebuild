Observed November 4, 2026. Evidence-only contribution for the successor to PR14;

## Findings that can inform implementation

1. **Keep multiple useful inventory shapes available.** The new sources describe
   both consolidating unwanted smalls and splitting one desirable item into
   useful smaller items. This supports preserving alternatives with materially
   different retained identities, denominations and set membership. It does not
   establish that maximum compactness, diversity or demand is always best.
2. **Use the actual feasible quote region, with cached canonical arithmetic.**
   Inspecter's advanced guide describes losing possible trades while leaving the
   game to look up unfamiliar comparable items. Precompute from the authorized
   immutable snapshot and inspect reachable alternatives before choosing a
   starting quote; do not add live in-trade HTTP, guess identities or adopt old
   creator prices. The video's reported lost trades are anecdotes, not a latency
   benchmark or a justification for weakening verification.
3. **Separate a counterparty's preference from a universal premium.** A source
   narrates taking smalls, later consolidating them and retaining an add for a
   future bundle. Another advises asking for adds only because a person may want
   the item. Preserve safe alternatives and bounded negotiation. These claims
   do not justify automatic extra concessions after silence, a set bonus, a
   regional trading advantage or an acceptance probability.
4. **Keep the strict +15 rule explicit when evaluating advice.** Several examples
   contain equal-value trades, small gains below15, paper losses to improve
   quality, event speculation or gifts. They cannot validate a profitable-every-
   leg automated strategy. A trade-wide concession ledger and unchanged native
   readiness gates are engineering safeguards; these videos do not prove their
   best numerical budgets or delays.
5. **Keep status, measured exits and uncertainty separate.** Creators assert
   liquidity differences, but also contradict one another, extrapolate from one
   price change and promise stable items never fall. Use sourced status/exposure
   as distinct facts; do not manufacture demand-to-cash multipliers or blanket
   Chroma/knife/set exclusions. The preserved publisher series, not these old
   quotes, supplies dated list-price evidence. Verified execution evidence remains
   absent.
