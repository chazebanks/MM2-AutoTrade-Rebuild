Counts are ceilings of `gap / assumed gain`, conditional on **every counted trade
being completed and retaining its quoted value**. They exclude declines, price
changes, unavailable counterparties and adverse selection. They are not promised
numbers of attempts or a forecast of attainable per-trade profit.

For the5,000 example, compounding a hypothetical1%,2% or5% gain on the **entire
portfolio per completed trade** would require116,58 or24 wins, respectively.
These are arithmetic upper-efficiency illustrations: a normal trade uses only
part of inventory, the4-copy constraint prevents trading an arbitrary whole
portfolio, and sustained5% portfolio gains are not established. The+15 floor
must also hold on each actual leg. Do not treat the compounding illustration as
a strategy benchmark.

Time depends on your observed completed/attempt rate and actual attempt rate:
`time = required completions / (attempts per hour × completion fraction)`.
Those inputs are unknown here. If the fraction is zero, the proposed route does
not progress. Inventory lockup and repeated resales of weak items add time; no
fixed deadline or “X days to goal” is defensible.

## Three plausible routes, none guaranteed

### 1. Broad liquid portfolio → gradual compact holdings

Use ordinary strictly profitable trades across the eligible universe. Preserve
some compact anchors and useful small denominations; review unknown demand and
new-event shocks. As holdings grow, seek profitable consolidation when available
without paying a prohibited numeric loss. This is the slowest-looking but most
constraint-compatible route. It can stall because sellers often want an overpay
for upgrades, which the bot must decline.

Human review tiers: first establish a verified2k–5k usable portfolio, then5k–10k,
then10k–15k+, then check whether an acceptable4-slot bundle is actually possible.
These are planning bands, not bot targets or proven optimal allocations. No named
stepping stone is necessary for the automated strategy.

### 2. Compact core plus a reserve of adds

Preserve several independently desirable medium/high-value items rather than
many low-demand duplicates. At current **base-list arithmetic only**, the
Traveler's Set12,700 plus Evergun3,450 totals16,150 across three component items;
two Traveler's Axes7,500 each plus Sakura1,150 also total16,150 across three copies.
These examples fit the conservative copy limit but **are not verified obtainable
deals**. The first set and the axes are Underpaid For; apparent16,150 coverage
does not mean a seller will prefer them or accept them for Gingerscope15,750.
They illustrate packing and quality risk, not recommended assets to accumulate.

### 3. Event-aware restraint rather than speculation

Avoid depending on newly released assets retaining launch values. The inspected
Embersickle publisher series fell from725 at its first October19 record to90,
with low85. That is not evidence that all event items crash or that every old
Christmas item rises. A route built on guaranteed seasonal appreciation is not
supported; preserving optionality and reviewing source freshness is preferable.

## Human-only final acquisition

1. Recheck the current primary quote, status, history and an actual seller's
   stated desired items. No runtime owner hunt or secret named-item preference.
2. Prepare a genuinely owned, tradable, source-verified compact bundle. Verify
   quality and quantities; list-equivalent value alone is insufficient.
3. Decide personally whether to offer an equal-value or premium exchange. For
   sensitivity only,5% or10% above today's15,750 is16,537.50 or17,325. Neither is a
   measured seller premium. This personal discretionary decision is outside the
   bot's strict+15 objective and must not alter its rules.
4. Use the normal in-game trade in one session. Verify the exact item/partner,
   Chroma/non-Chroma identity, actual slot contents and inventory receipt. Reject
   loan/trust/offsite/multi-round promises. Keep a reserve if losing all usable
   inventory would undermine further trading.
5. Treat the acquisition as a human decision and record it separately from
   bot-performance evaluation. Do not make the trader stop, prefer the item or
   seek its owners afterward.
